## Ch25 · Modern Features

Recent C++ standards added features that cut the repetition from older code and make intent clearer. This chapter covers the ones used most often in everyday code: `auto` and structured bindings, which name and unpack values, and `std::optional`, which states that a result may be missing. The chapter draws on *A Tour of C++* for its overview of the language's newer features.

The steps are:

* [auto and structured bindings](#goto/01-auto-and-bindings): letting the compiler work out types, and unpacking values into named parts.
* [optional](#goto/02-optional): returning a value or nothing, and reading it safely.
* [Summary](#goto/03-summary): a recap of the chapter and a short quiz. The homework is listed under the chapter.

## Ch15 · Efficiency

Two programs can give the same answer at very different costs. This chapter covers how to describe that cost as the input grows, and how to check it by counting operations. The Fibonacci example shows a simple recursive definition that repeats an enormous amount of work, and the steps that fix it.

The steps are:

* [Understanding Big-O](#goto/00-understanding-big-o): what an algorithm is, what complexity measures, and the common growth rates.
* [Counting operations](#goto/01-counting-operations): counting the work of one loop and of nested loops, and what Big-O names.
* [Fibonacci](#goto/02-fibonacci): counting the calls of naive, memoized, and iterative versions of the same calculation.
* [Summary](#goto/03-summary): a recap of the chapter and a short quiz. The homework is listed under the chapter.

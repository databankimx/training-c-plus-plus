## Ch01 · Getting Started

This chapter gets you from nothing to a running C++ program. You'll check your setup, write the smallest complete program there is, and learn the pieces it's made from. Along the way you'll see how the runner turns your source into a program, so that when something goes wrong later, you'll know where to look.

The steps are:

* [Set up your workstation](#goto/00-setup): a check that the compiler and the runner are in place. This step has no code.
* [Hello, world](#goto/01-hello-world): a program that prints two lines, and the parts of a C++ program.
* [Namespaces](#goto/02-namespaces): the `std::` prefix, and when to use `using namespace std`.
* [Comments](#goto/03-comments): how to leave notes for the people who read your code.
* [Summary](#goto/04-summary): a recap of the chapter and a short quiz. The homework is listed under the chapter.

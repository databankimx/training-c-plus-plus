## Ch04 · Arrays

Until now, each value has had its own variable. This chapter introduces the array, which stores a list of values of one type under a single name. You'll learn how to declare an array, how to refer to each element by its index, and the ways to fill one when you create it.

The steps are:

* [Declaring arrays](#goto/01-declaring-arrays): declaring an array, indexing from zero, and what goes wrong when an index is past the end.
* [Initializing arrays](#goto/02-initializing-arrays): partly filled arrays, leaving out the size, and finding the length with `std::size`.
* [Multidimensional arrays](#goto/03-multidimensional-arrays): grids with a row and a column index, and how to initialize them.
* [Summary](#goto/04-summary): a recap of the chapter and a short quiz. The homework is listed under the chapter.
